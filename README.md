Experience a fresh take on the 4-player chess variant game model, with this unique toroidal board. Navigate treacherous 'mountains' and 'oceans' as you outwit opponents in a strategic battle for territory-supremacy, denoted by color - and the player can choose their own color, so this game is a bit like painting with chess pieces. Perfect for chess enthusiasts seeking a unique-yet-familiar, and challenging twist on the ancient, ever-evolving game, as queen promotion is only possible when two pawns safely occupy the mountain-valley squares for 3 of their own turns, or 12 player-turns total between the four players. Geography and chess in balance and harmony - and with this board style, it is going to be a snap of the fingers to roll out later versions with bigger boards to suit any number of players.
